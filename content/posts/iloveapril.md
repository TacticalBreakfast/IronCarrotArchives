Hello, and welcome to another TacticalBreakfast blog post. I don't write these as much as I thought I would, although I maybe shouldn't have been surprised by that. I have a lot of ideas, but I'm also very picky. If I start writing one and it doesn't come out right, I simply won't publish it. But maybe that's for the better because if I just vomited out whatever came to mind, no one would read the mess. I at least get to maintain the illusion of quality!

Anyway, that leads me to my disclaimer. What follows contains nothing useful. This is just a ramble about my own thoughts as I consider what actually matters to me in terms of a waifu and what I actually like about mine. There's no gameplay discussion here. There's probably nothing at all useful to anyone but me. I'm not even sure why you're here reading this! But I do appreciate it if you haven't already closed the tab.

Anyway, my love of a certain fast-redeploying bunny is well known at this point, but if you're a newer reader to my space, I dearly love April, and have for a long time. It's come up in articles and fics, and I've commissioned a dozen or so artworks of her, and even two full figures, and soon to be a plush too. Hell, she's the inspiration for the icon of this very website! This is particularly relevant now with Orchid2's release, and no shortage of people have chimed in to remind me that my beloved is now powercrept (as if any 5★ matters these days, but I digress).

This doesn't actually bother me at all, but it has made me consider a few things. One of the things I like to say when the topic comes up is that a large part of why I love April is her kit. The number I often quote is 70:30. That is, 70% of it is because of her kit, while just 30% is the rest. In fact, if I'm honest, she's a rather boring character, albeit one that does slot neatly into my own preferences.

There's two major parts of her kit design, the FRD aspect and the stealth aspect. The FRD aspect is the easy one, and I've always liked units with reduced redeployments. Projekt Red, Gravel, Phantom. Even the more meta ones like Texas the Omertosa and Kirin R Yato (although I don't like the latter much, hint hint) are M6 Mod6 max level units on my roster! It's just a dynamic playstyle I enjoy where every map and every use is different.

The stealth aspect is a more personal preference. I have *always* gravitated towards stealth gameplay. I'm the sort of player who is always the rogue in D&D. I was a rogue in Everquest, the OG of no-life MMOs. I played the fuck out of Splinter Cell back in the day. I'm the sort of player who thinks the old Assassin's Creed games were peak. I was a stealth archer in Skyrim before it was cool!

So when I say something like April feels like she was made just for me, this is why. She just brings together themes and playstyles I like. But of course, that's not all either. I like Red, but I've never considered her a waifu. That sort of personality just doesn't do it for me, and if she were real, I'd no doubt find myself exceedingly annoyed when dealing with her (good thing I only play a Doktah rather than have to be one). However, while I always acknowledged there was a personality and visual design component to April that I also liked (the 30% part) which completed the picture, I have always tended to downplay the importance a bit.

Which brings me, finally, to Violet Mizutsune Orchid, who has made me wonder if I've underestimated that 30%. To start, I absolutely plan to roll for and use Orchid2, including pots (although maybe only to the redeploy pot, gotta save for Thumpy). I don't actually roll for many 6★s on my main account since the meta grind bores me, but there are some I absolutely adore. Orchid will be one of them too. I do really enjoy the gameplay!

But, there's something about Orchid, both the unit and the character, that I just... don't like. She's always been one of my least favorite 3★s. In fact, I kinda dislike the entirety of A6. They've always felt a bit out of place to me, like they don't quite belong or were designed for a slightly different game. I can't quite place my finger on why either, but Orchid especially has never been appealing to me. That character archetype just does not do it for me at all.

Maybe worse though is the visual design. I've never talked about it much because I tend not to make a big deal about the thematic fit of collabs, although I do like to praise it when it fits perfectly like Rainbow 6 or Delta Force. They're a commercial exercise after all, intended to primarily expand the game audience. It's just part of the gacha space. However, that said, I do find the MH aesthetic to be incredibly out of place in Arknights. Even the base Ave Mujica band skins fit better (as long as you ignore the fact they're non-fighting teenagers), but at least they have thematic skins. MH units to me just stick out like a sore thumb, and I'm always aware of how out of place they are when I use them. I can at least lie to myself with the Mujica units! Maybe ironically, but April's base design is incongruous with this aspect as well, and although she acknowledges it in a voice line, it still felt out of place. But like the Mujica girls, her skin is a perfect fit, and incidentally, also came out at the same time, so it's something I've never considered much¹.

And then there's a thematic decision behind Orchid2 that I dislike as a personal preference as well, even if there's no functional difference. Take-Off, although a perfectly fine mechanic in principle, is *not* stealth. The functional effect is mostly the same, but it doesn't scratch that same itch that stealth does. In fact, it kinda does the opposite, because the only reason there's functional immunity with it is because HG couldn't be bothered to code ranged enemies to target them. The stealth is a byproduct of half-assed code!

These thoughts have made me wonder about how much I actually weigh that 30%. Granted, you could argue that the stealth vs Take-Off part belongs in the 70% instead, and also granted there's a rarity difference to consider as well. I've always loved 5★s more, even before it was a dedicated niche. But still, would I love April 70% as much if everything else about her was this unappealing to me?

I've thought about this a lot lately, and I don't have a good answer. I suppose if I reframe the question as something like, "Do I love Projekt Red 70% as much as April?" then it makes a little more sense. But it's really hard to divine out how much all these different aspects of something really matter to you. Maybe there's a lesson here that isn't actually related to gacha at all. Maybe it's something like seeing the forest for the trees. You can talk about the individual parts, and they can be important to separate and distill, but it's the total package that matters.

Or maybe I'm just full of myself, and spending far too much time thinking about a fictional png. Who knows. In any case, April is safe as my waifu for a good long while still.

¹ Not that anyone asked, but since I’m already just talking about myself and my wife here, I have April wearing her normal outfit (E1) as an assistant, but the skin in stage.
