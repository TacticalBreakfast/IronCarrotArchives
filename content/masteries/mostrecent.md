### Violet Mizutsune Orchid

{{< mastery-table-enhanced id="1048_orchd2" rows="S2M3,S+,S+|S3M3,B+,B,Breakpoint - S3M1|S1M3,None,None" >}}

Amid the sea of hyper-meta units that have been the norm of late, Violet Mizutsune Orchid is a somewhat more novel unit. The non-Specialist FRD space is an underutilized one that HG rarely treads on (April was SIX years ago!). That makes Orchid2 one of the more unique units in recent memory, and the flexibility she brings, both in terms of where she can be deployed and how fast, makes her a great tool to have on your roster. This comes at a small cost to her DPS, which tends not to be as crazy as other recent units, however the flexibility she brings allows that DPS to be better placed exactly where it's needed, meaning she's still an exceptionally powerful unit, albeit one that requires a bit more effort.

Before diving into her Masteries, you should actually first start with her base Module. Although some of it comes from her Talent, which separates her from the other ARC-X users, the 25 seconds of redeployment time her mod1 provides is vitally important to her flexibility. However, her upgrades, although still powerful and you should plan to get them, are less vital, so can be saved for after primary Mastery, which is itself important.

With her base ARC-X in hand, her primary Mastery, and only for many, will be her S2 which plays best with her reduced redeployment. With it, she Takes-Off, where she becomes immune to most damage and targeting, leaving her safe to rain down a hail of arrows over a wide area. Don't be fooled by the reference to firing X number of arrows which to many would imply a single-target. It's actually a True-AoE (don't ask me how that works) with the number of arrows representing the instances of damage. Importantly, she gets a free activation that triggers immediately, after which her second charge is usually ready, allowing for a very fast burst of damage.

Mastery is important due to the initial SP cost improvements, which, despite what may appear to be a small gain of only 3 SP, make a large difference since at S2M3 she can functionally activate twice in a row, while S2SL7 has a frustratingly long gap that leaves her very vulnerable. That's vitally important for a hit-and-run (or rather hit-from-safety) unit like Orchid2. The behavior here is actually fairly unusual. Despite being charge-based, she still suffers from SP-lockout, meaning she doesn't gain additional SP during that free activation. However, there is a short delay before it where she does generate SP. At SL7 this leaves her waiting around 4 seconds for her second activation, but it's only around 1 at M3. Since she is reliant on the Take-Off portion for safety, that ends up being a massive gap. That alone is worth a high priority Mastery consideration, but it's worth noting that the damage gain itself is fairly average, although on a relatively low DPH skill like this, every bit of ATK does matter.

After that, you should likely consider her ARC-X Module level 3 before any secondary skills, as the mod3 upgrade is important and both alternative skills are somewhat flawed. If you enjoy the playstyle enough to consider one though, it gets a bit complicated, but the fundamental suggestion is to probably not strongly fuss about it unless you have a specific need or a lot of resources. Most people looking for a second should consider her S3 first. However, the 3 second draw-time on it is very awkward, it takes unreasonably long for her second shot to be ready, and straight-line ranges are rarely good. In fact, it's the fatal flaw of the very worst 6★ Sniper, Fartooth, although Orchid2 at least has a lot of flexibility in her deployment which mitigates it somewhat. The damage isn't even particularly compelling for the awkwardness either, except against the large multi-tile bosses, which are a relative minority. However, it does have uses now and then, and the Masteries are fairly strong. In addition to the typical damage increase, there's a breakpoint at S3M1 that allows her to deploy with a charge ready, and another at S3M3 which increases the Force, although that part is easy to forget is even there. Still, creative players and those who want a bit more out of Orchid2 will find it to have some value.

Of that latter group, some may actually prefer her S1, although many units these days do it far better. It's basically her AFK skill, and can output some pretty reasonable and consistent damage, entirely hands off. However, that's a pretty pointless thing on a Fast-Redeploy unit. It's simply not what Orchid2 is good at or used for, and even if you are an extreme AFK player, you'll be better off with almost anyone else since virtually every new 6★ these days has an AFK or low effort skill of some kind. She doesn't even get an expanded range on it! However, it does have fairly strong gains, and even has an unusual M2 breakpoint which grants an extra charge, although it isn't labeled here due to the low value of the skill. There are a few people out there who will find value in the skill, but most, even the AFK players, will find better value for their resources elsewhere.

### Zinogre S Catapult

{{< mastery-table-enhanced id="1049_catap2" rows="S2M3,C-,C|S1M3,None,None" >}}

While Violet Mizutsune Orchid explores a relatively unused niche, her 5★ counterpart is a far weaker showing, illustrating little of the potential behind the Mercenary archetype. DP is an underutilized resource, but it's not free either, and Zinogre S Catapult simply doesn't do enough to justify that drain. She provides only damage, and fairly weak damage at that, and there are many 5★ Guards out there who will do more without the drawback, ultimately meaning few should consider raising her at all. However, if you're one of the few who isn't traumatized by her base version’s 3★ recruit line (it didn't use to be as fast, and took forever to get through on every recruit), she does enough to at least be functional.

If that's you, there's really not a standout skill, and you should probably plan to do both, or pick based on your planned use. Her S2 is the more unique one, with the greater potential to do something unique. Meanwhile, her S1 will probably be the better "daily-driver" type, but it's much less interesting too, with a much lower ceiling since it only offers some fairly minor hit-count utility, which the rarity already has in abundance. For that reason, the grades here give a slight favor to her S2.

Starting with that, the key feature worth noting is that it can be spammed. That's a use that's easy to miss since a lot of her kit revolves around powering up with additional charges (which S2 does as well), but the best use here is actually that you can activate it after only 2 SP, then immediately do the equipment activation for the big burst. Supposing you can spare 15 DP each time at least. That restriction is a daunting one, since skill spam by its nature will drain your DP very fast. Still, there are a handful of places it can be used to great effect, and the rarity doesn't have many units who can do anything similar. For the strength of gains themselves, they're slightly above average. It's a decent DPS gain, but not a crazy one either.

Her S1 meanwhile is only really useful with the extended duration her charges give. The 20 DP cost for a single activation is staggering, and the default duration of only 4 seconds is not worth the effect. However, with a full (or near full) complement of charges, it can output some decent DPS against multiple targets, with a more reasonable, although still relatively short, duration. If you like Catapult enough to regularly use her, this will make for a passable everyday skill, and the transition to Arts damage even resolves DPH issues. However, the cost is still staggering and the DPS is overall nothing special. Again, the gains here are slightly above average but not crazy. They're worthwhile if you actually intend to use her.
